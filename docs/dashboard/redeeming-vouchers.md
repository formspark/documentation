# Redeeming vouchers

A voucher adds submissions to a workspace. Admins and editors can redeem one.

## Steps

1. Open the workspace's `Settings` screen
2. Under `Billing`, press `Redeem` on the `Voucher` row
3. Enter your voucher and press `Redeem`
