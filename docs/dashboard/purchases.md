# Purchases and invoices

A workspace keeps a list of the bundles bought for it, each with its invoice.
Only workspace admins can see it, since an invoice carries the buyer's name and
address. See [roles](/dashboard/inviting-team-members#roles).

## Finding an invoice

1. Open the workspace's `Settings` screen
2. Under `Billing`, press `View` on the `Purchases` row
3. Press `Invoice` next to the purchase

Each purchase shows its date, amount and status. Older purchases offer a
`Receipt` instead of an invoice. Purchases made before April 2023 are not
listed.

## Adding your business details

To receive a business invoice, add your company name, address and VAT number
at checkout.

If you missed it, you can still add them afterwards:

- For a purchase with an invoice, press `Billing details` to open your billing
  portal and edit them there. Only the admin who paid can open it.
- For a purchase with a receipt, open the receipt and add them there.

## Refunds

A purchase refunded in full, or reversed through a chargeback, takes back the
submissions from it that the workspace still holds. When no other purchase
remains, the workspace returns to the free plan. Paid features such as the
autoresponder pause and keep their settings, and resume once the workspace
buys again.

A partial refund leaves the submissions in place.
