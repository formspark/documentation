# Purchases and invoices

Workspace admins find every purchase, with its invoice, in the workspace's
`Settings`, under `Billing` in `Purchases`. Press `View`, then `Invoice` next to
the purchase. Older purchases offer a `Receipt` instead.

To get a business invoice, add your company name, address and VAT number at
checkout. If you missed it, press `Billing details` on the purchase, or add
them on the receipt.
